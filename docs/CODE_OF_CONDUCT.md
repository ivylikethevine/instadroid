# Code of conduct

instadroid is a small project, and this is meant to keep it a place where anyone can report a bug,
ask a question or send a change without having to brace themselves first.

## What's expected

- Be respectful and assume good faith, including toward people who are new to Android, redroid or
  Python.
- Give and take technical criticism about the work, not the person.
- Be patient: this is maintained on a best-effort basis.
- Respect privacy. This project handles logged-in accounts and other people's posts. Never share
  someone else's credentials, session data, screenshots or scraped content in issues, pull requests
  or fixtures without their consent, and strip personal data from any logs or dumps you post.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or unwelcome sexual attention or imagery.
- Discrimination or demeaning remarks about anyone's identity or background.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing anyone's private information (addresses, accounts, photos) without explicit permission.
- Using the project's spaces to coordinate harassment of, or unwanted surveillance of, a particular
  person, including through the accounts this tool scrapes.
- Continuing any of the above after being asked to stop.

## Scope

This applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, the
security advisory tracker) and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Contact the maintainer, [@ivylikethevine](https://github.com/ivylikethevine), privately, using the
contact details on that GitHub profile. For a security vulnerability, use the private process in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead. Reports are handled confidentially, and the privacy of the
person reporting is respected.

## Enforcement

The maintainer decides what counts as a violation and how to respond, in proportion to what
happened: from a private note asking for a change in behavior, to hiding or deleting comments, to a
temporary or permanent ban from the project's spaces. Contributions that break this code may be
edited, closed or reverted.
