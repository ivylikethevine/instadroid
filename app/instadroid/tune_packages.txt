# Packages the device tuning disables (`pm disable-user --user 0`): apps the scraper never touches that
# would otherwise sit resident in this container, where lmkd never reclaims anything (CLAUDE.md, "Memory";
# docs/INCIDENTS.md, "Reducing idle memory"). One home for two readers: instadroid/tune.py (on the app's
# first connect) and scripts/tune-android.sh (by hand). One per line; blank and # lines are skipped.
#
# Never add com.android.packageinstaller: PackageManagerService needs exactly one enabled installer and
# crash-loops system_server on the next cold boot without it (docs/INCIDENTS.md). Left enabled on purpose:
# com.android.settings, com.android.provision / com.android.managedprovisioning (setup flows that may have
# to run again after a /data/system reset), and anything telephony/Bluetooth/secure-element-related.
# Test any change here against a full cold restart, not just the booted instance you disabled it on.
